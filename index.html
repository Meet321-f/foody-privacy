Privacy Policy

Application: Foody

Developer: Meet Tailor

Last Updated: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Foody collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use our mobile application and related services, including account authentication, AI recipe generation, recipe discovery, community recipe sharing, comments, reports, favorites, feedback, and in-app advertising.

1. Who We Are

Foody is a recipe discovery, meal planning, and generative AI cooking assistant application developed by Meet Tailor ("we", "us", or "our"). Our mission is to help home cooks discover recipes, plan meals, and generate unique culinary ideas based on ingredients they have on hand.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly to Foody

  • Account Profile: Display name and email address when you sign up, sign in, or update your profile.
  • AI Recipe Prompts: Text descriptions of ingredients, dietary preferences, or cravings you submit to Chef Foody AI.
  • User-Created Recipes: Recipe titles, descriptions, ingredients, step-by-step instructions, servings, prep time, categories, and optional recipe images you choose to publish.
  • Community Comments: Comments and discussions you post on recipes.
  • Moderation Reports: Recipe reports you submit to report inappropriate or infringing content, including the report reason.
  • Feedback: Feedback messages, ratings, and issue categories you voluntarily send to our support team.

Information collected automatically by Foody

  • Authentication Identifiers: Your unique Firebase Authentication user ID (UID) to securely identify your account session.
  • App Activity & Preferences: Your saved favorite recipes, AI recipe generation history, and daily AI recipe generation counts.
  • Technical & Diagnostic Data: Error logs, system crash reports, and service connectivity failures used strictly for troubleshooting and app stability.
  • Notification Status: Permission state allowing the app to send optional recipe completion notifications.

Information processed by Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

Foody integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) to serve banner advertisements and rewarded video advertisements. AdMob operates independently and may automatically collect:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID), device model, operating system version, and mobile network carrier.
  • Telemetry & Usage Data: IP address (coarse location), ad views, ad clicks, rewarded ad completions, and diagnostic metrics.
  • Ad Personalization: Depending on your device settings and local regulations, ads may be personalized or non-personalized.

Note: Foody does not sell your personal data. We do not store your Advertising ID on our private servers.

Foody does NOT collect sensitive personal data

  • No precise GPS location tracking.
  • No credit card, debit card, or financial banking information.
  • No address book, contacts list, or phone directory access.
  • No health records, medical data, or biometric data.

3. Device Permissions We Request

Foody requests only the minimum permissions necessary to deliver app features:

  • Camera Access (android.permission.CAMERA): Optional permission requested if you choose to use the camera to take a photo of ingredients in your fridge or capture a picture for a recipe or profile. Photos captured for ingredient analysis are converted to temporary data on-device and are not permanently retained on external servers unless you voluntarily publish them with a custom recipe.
  • Photo Library / Media Access: Optional permission allowing you to select an existing photo from your device gallery for your profile avatar or recipe card.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Optional permission to alert you when an AI recipe generation task completes in the background.
  • Haptics & Vibration: Used to provide tactile feedback during interactive app gestures.

You can enable, disable, or revoke any of these permissions at any time through your Android device settings.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To create, authenticate, and manage your Foody user account.
  • To synchronize your profile, favorite recipes, and AI history across your devices.
  • To process your recipe prompts and return customized culinary suggestions and instructions.
  • To enforce daily free recipe quotas and credit bonus recipe generations when you watch optional rewarded ads.
  • To display user-submitted community recipes and comments.
  • To review content moderation reports and prevent spam, abuse, or harmful material.
  • To analyze technical diagnostics, resolve crashes, and improve overall app performance.

5. Where Your Data Is Stored

  • Firebase Authentication (Google Cloud): Manages secure user sign-in tokens, email credentials, and authentication state.
  • Google Cloud Firestore: Cloud database storing user profile records (users/{uid}), user favorites (favorites), AI history (ai_history), public custom recipes (coustome_recipes), comments (comments), content moderation reports (reports), user feedback (feedback), and daily AI rate limits (ai_recipe_daily_limits).
  • Firebase Storage: Foody currently processes images directly in-app or via external links; personal image files are not hosted on active Firebase Storage buckets.
  • Local Device Storage: Foody stores local favorites, cached recipes, onboarding flags, and AI history on your device using SQLite (foody.db) and AsyncStorage for quick offline access.

6. Third-Party Services & SDK Disclosures

Foody partners with trusted service providers to deliver specialized functionality:

  • Google AdMob (Google LLC): Delivers in-app banner ads and rewarded video advertisements. AdMob processes device identifiers and interaction data to serve ads. You can manage or reset your advertising preferences anytime in Android under Settings > Google > Ads. For full details, see Google Privacy Policy and Google Ad Technologies.
  • Google Sign-In (Google LLC): Enables quick sign-in using your Google Account, sharing approved basic profile data (name, email, avatar).
  • Firebase Services (Google LLC): Provides authentication, cloud database (Firestore), and secure Cloud Functions backend hosting.
  • OpenRouter & AI Model Providers: Foody Cloud Functions communicate securely with OpenRouter (https://openrouter.ai/), which routes requests to state-of-the-art language models from OpenAI (such as GPT-4o-mini) and Google Gemini (such as Gemini-2.0-flash-lite). Only ingredient and culinary prompt text is sent; no personal user credentials or identity records are shared with AI providers.
  • TheMealDB: An open culinary database used to search standard recipes, ingredient filters, and public meal categories.
  • Expo Application Services (EAS Updates): Powers over-the-air (OTA) application updates, processing runtime versions and anonymous update IDs to deliver bug fixes without requiring a full APK reinstallation.

7. AI Features & Food Safety Notice

When you use Chef Foody AI, your recipe prompt and ingredients list are transmitted securely through our backend to generate cooking suggestions.

⚠️ Food Safety & Dietary Disclaimer

AI-generated recipes are generated algorithmically for informational culinary exploration. AI output may contain errors, omission of ingredients, or inaccurate cooking times. Always independently verify food freshness, safe internal cooking temperatures (especially for meat, poultry, and seafood), and potential food allergens before preparing or consuming any meal.

8. Public Community & Sharing

When you voluntarily submit a recipe for publication, that recipe (including title, ingredients, instructions, and your public display name) becomes visible to other Foody users. Comments posted on recipes are public. Moderation reports and feedback are private and visible only to the app administration.

9. Data Retention Policy

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy:

Data Category Retention Period
Account & Profile Data Retained while your account is active. Permanently deleted upon verified account deletion.
Favorites & AI Recipe History Retained until deleted by you in the app or cleared during account deletion.
Community Recipes & Comments Retained while the recipe remains published. Anonymized or removed upon user account deletion request.
Daily AI Quota Records Automatically expire on a rolling daily basis.
Diagnostic & Server Logs Maintained for up to 90 days for security monitoring and troubleshooting, then deleted.

10. In-App Account Deletion

Foody provides a direct, permanent account deletion option inside the app:

  1. Open Foody and tap the Settings tab.
  2. Scroll down to the Account Actions section.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm your request.

Deleting your account permanently deletes your authentication credentials, Firestore profile record, favorites, AI recipe history, and local cached data.

11. Request Account and Personal Data Deletion (Web Protocol)

Web-Based Data Deletion for Uninstalled Users

If you have uninstalled the application, cannot access your device, or prefer to request data deletion via email, you may submit a formal data deletion request without signing in:

  • Contact Email: meettailor.dev@gmail.com
  • Subject Line: Data Deletion Request
  • Required Information: Please provide your registered account email address and your display name as shown in Foody.
  • Verification Requirement: To protect account privacy and prevent fraudulent requests, the deletion request must be sent directly from the email address registered with your Foody account.
  • Processing Timeframe: All verified data deletion requests are processed within thirty (30) calendar days of receipt.
  • Scope of Deletion: Upon verification, your Firebase authentication record, Firestore profile document, personal favorites, AI history, and daily rate limit entries will be permanently purged from our active systems.
  • Legal Exceptions: Certain limited records (such as content moderation abuse reports or fraud prevention logs) may be retained where strictly required by law or legitimate security obligations.
